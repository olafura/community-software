# Code of Conduct

Our full expectations are in the [charter](COMMUNITY.md). This page covers what that means day to day, and what happens when something goes wrong.

## What we expect

- **Welcome people.** Be patient with newcomers, beginner questions, and people writing in a second language.
- **Assume good faith.** Text loses tone. Read generously, and ask before assuming the worst.
- **Critique the work, not the person.** Question designs, point out edge cases, and explain how a problem affects you.
- **Respect boundaries.** Respect people's time, their "no", and their need to step away.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, threats, or discrimination of any kind
- Insults, belittling, or sarcasm aimed at people
- Repeatedly pinging or chasing people across channels after they've asked you to stop
- Sharing someone's private information without their consent
- Sexualized language or imagery

## Where this applies

This applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, chat, forums, events, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Write to **conduct@community-software.org**. A small group of volunteers reads every report in confidence. If a team member is involved, they will step aside from handling your report.

You don't need to be certain something broke the rules before you report it. We'd rather hear about it.

## What happens next

We respond proportionately, and usually in this order:

1. **A conversation.** A private, friendly note. Most issues end here.
2. **A formal warning.** If the behavior continues.
3. **Time away.** A temporary removal from project spaces. If the behavior is serious or keeps happening, the removal can be permanent.

Serious harm, such as threats or harassment, can skip straight to step 3.

We will tell the person who reported it what we decided, while respecting everyone's privacy.
