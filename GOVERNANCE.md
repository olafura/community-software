# Governance

We keep governance light. These principles come from our [charter](COMMUNITY.md).

## How decisions are made

**The people doing the work guide the decisions.** If you're writing the code, docs, design, or tests for something, you have a strong voice in how it's done.

Most decisions happen through **lazy consensus**:

1. Propose something in an issue or pull request.
2. Give people reasonable time to respond. For anything significant, that's at least a week.
3. If nobody raises a concern, go ahead.

If people disagree, discuss it in the open and look for an option everyone can live with. If you still can't agree, the maintainers decide and write down why.

## Roles

- **Contributors** are everyone who helps in any way, whether by reporting, testing, translating, designing, documenting, or coding.
- **Maintainers** review and merge changes, triage issues, and look after the project's direction. Being a maintainer is a responsibility, not a rank.

## Becoming a maintainer

There's no application form. People who have contributed steadily and helped others are invited by the existing maintainers. Any kind of contribution counts, not only code.

## Stepping back

Anyone can step back at any time, for any reason. Maintainers who have been inactive for a while move to *emeritus* status. Their past work is credited, and they're always welcome to return.

## Changing these documents

Changes to the charter or governance follow lazy consensus with at least two weeks for comment. Open a pull request.
