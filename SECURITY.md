# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **don't** open a public issue for a security problem. Write to **security@community-software.org** with:

- what the problem is and where it occurs
- the steps to reproduce it, or a proof of concept
- the impact, as you understand it

We'll confirm we got your report as soon as a volunteer can. We'll keep you updated while we work on a fix. We'll credit you when the fix is released, unless you'd rather stay anonymous.

## Disclosure

We ask that you give us reasonable time to release a fix before you share details publicly. We'll agree on a date with you.
