# Getting help

Real people answer questions here, in their spare time. There's no guaranteed response time, but there's a good chance someone can help.

## Where to ask

- **Questions and "how do I…?"**: open a discussion rather than an issue.
- **Something is broken**: open a bug report using the issue form.
- **An idea or request**: open a feature request, and tell us how you could help.
- **Security problems**: please don't post them publicly. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Conduct concerns**: write to [conduct@community-software.org](mailto:conduct@community-software.org).

## Getting a good answer

- Search first. Someone may have asked already.
- Say what you're trying to do, not only what failed.
- Include your version, platform, and any error messages.
- Once you find the answer, post it. It will help the next person.

## A note on expectations

This software is free and comes with no warranty or service agreement. Nobody is obligated to help, but people often do because they care. Kindness and patience go a long way.
